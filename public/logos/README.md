# Third-party logos

`mapillary-logo.png` is the Mapillary logo on its dark plate (`mapillary_logo_dark@2x.png`), taken unchanged from Mapillary's official press kit ([mapillary/mapillary_press v0.0.4, `logos.zip`](https://github.com/mapillary/mapillary_press/releases/tag/v0.0.4)) and only scaled down to 48 px high. The Mapillary Terms (§ 11) ask apps that show Mapillary imagery to display the Mapillary logo and link back to mapillary.com; the Street Level credit does exactly that. The logo follows Mapillary's logo usage guidelines (colours, spacing and proportions unchanged).
